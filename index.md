# Take your pleasure *seriously.*

Prototype is the typography club at SCAD Savannah. We draw letters by hand, set them by machine, and build them by rule. Every major is welcome.

## Every week

- **Club meeting** — Tuesdays, 7:30 pm, Adler 133
- **Calligraphy at Cargo** — Saturdays, 11 am, Cargo, River House. Drop in. This quarter: the Humanist hand, through Week 9.

## Show up, collect

Every Prototype event earns a sticker on your loyalty card. Regulars earn limited editions, starting with a zine.

## Elsewhere

Workshops, calls and work in progress go up first on [Instagram @pr_t_type](https://www.instagram.com/pr_t_type/).
